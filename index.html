Julien Béguinot

I am a postdoctoral reseracher at UCLouvain in the Crypto Group under the supervision of Pr. François-Xavier Standaert.

Before that I was a PhD candidate in Télécom Paris (Institut Polytechnique de Paris) under the supervision of Pr. Olivier Rioul and Pr. Sylvain Guilley.

I am focused on information theory and its application to security with a focus on side channel analysis. However, I am intereted in applied mathematics and probabilities in general.

You can find most of my articles on [DBLP].
